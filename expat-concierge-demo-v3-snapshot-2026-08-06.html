Multi-Agent Architecture

Every user question passes through a layered pipeline of specialised agents and services before reaching GPT-4o. No answer is generated without policy validation, access control, confidence scoring, and decision framing.

Request flow

Access Control check → Policy Agent Router → Specialist Agent → Decision Engine → Confidence Engine → Chat Agent (context build) → GPT-4o → streamed response

Orchestration

Policy Agent Router

Single entry point for every question. Reads the user's assignment type and routes to the correct specialist. Enforces type safety — a mismatched agent is caught and the Generic fallback is activated automatically.

Chat Agent

Validates specialist output before trusting it, then assembles the full LLM context block — injecting guidance, decision type, confidence score, risk level, missing information, and escalation targets into the GPT-4o prompt.

Specialist Agents

LTA Specialist

Long-Term Assignment advisor. Handles the most complex assignment type with escalation logic for tax, visa, payroll, and exceptions. Raises profile-specific flags for pending visa, school search for children, and pet relocation.

STA Specialist

Short-Term Assignment advisor. Aware that STA extensions require explicit approval — flags these automatically. Covers temporary living, per diems, settling-in services, and split payroll scenarios.

IA Specialist

International Appointment advisor. Handles the most legally sensitive assignment type — local entity implications, employment contract scope, compensation package governance, and right-to-work risks.

Commuter Specialist

Commuter assignment advisor. The only agent that handles cross-border social security, dual-jurisdiction tax, and border crossing authorizations. Flags travel frequency and accommodation patterns.

Generic Fallback

Last resort when no specialist can safely handle the request. Always escalates to the GM Team, flags reduced confidence, and provides general market practice guidance only — never makes policy-specific claims without evidence.

Support Services

Decision Engine

Classifies every response into one of five states: Clear, Conditional, Insufficient, Needs Review, or Out of Scope. Drives how the Chat Agent frames the final answer.

Confidence Engine

Scores confidence (high / medium / low) and risk level based on evidence volume, specialist quality, and profile flags. Starts at 100% and deducts per signal — both scores injected into the LLM prompt.

Policy Access Control

Hard boundary enforcement. Blocks any request that references a policy the user is not authorized for. An LTA expat can never access STA, IA, or Commuter policy content — regardless of how the question is phrased.

Automatic Escalation

When a question touches a sensitive topic — tax, immigration, exceptions, or social security — the system automatically flags it for the right specialist team. This happens before the AI answers, so the expat always receives both the AI guidance and a clear recommendation to confirm with the appropriate team.

Intent

The goal is not to block the conversation — it is to protect the expat. The AI still provides useful, policy-grounded guidance while making clear that certain decisions require human confirmation. No sensitive topic is answered in isolation.

The Challenge

For expats

Global assignments are complex. Expats face dozens of simultaneous processes — visa applications, housing searches, school enrollment, tax registration — with little centralised guidance and no single source of truth. Stress and errors are common.

For PBP and GM teams

Every assignment generates hundreds of repetitive questions across email, Teams, and calls. Deadlines are missed when task ownership is unclear. Policy guidance is inconsistent, creating compliance risk and eroding expat trust.

The opportunity

A policy-aware AI concierge eliminates repetitive Q&A, enforces assignment boundaries, tracks every action item with clear ownership, and gives expats the guided experience they need to succeed — without adding headcount.

Core Capabilities

AI-Powered Chat

Instant answers to any relocation question, grounded in company policy and scoped to the expat's assignment type. Responses are personalised to the expat's profile — family situation, visa status, housing needs, tax concerns.

Action Log & Task Tracking

34 pre-loaded tasks per assignment with deadlines, owners (Expat, GM Team, Vialto, K2, Net Expat), and completion status. Nothing falls through the cracks. Overdue items are highlighted with alerts.

Multi-Profile Management

Any number of expat profiles managed simultaneously. PBP admins can simulate any user, view their full action log, and switch context in one click. One platform for all active assignments.

Proactive Alerts

Tasks due within 7 days surface automatically in the chat header. Overdue tasks are injected into the AI context so every answer is action-aware — not just policy-aware.

Policy Enforcement

Assignment boundaries are strictly enforced. An LTA expat cannot receive STA benefit guidance. Every response is explainable and auditable, reducing legal and compliance exposure.

Suggested Questions

Chat opens with personalised question chips based on the expat's exact profile — visa status, housing, schooling, tax equalization. Action log chips surface overdue and upcoming tasks as conversational prompts.

The Assignment Journey

Five stages from first login to PBP oversight — each step building on the last to guide the expat through the full assignment lifecycle.

01 — Profile Loaded

The system reads the expat's assignment type, stage (pre / during / post), family situation, host and home countries, department, and expected move date. Every subsequent interaction is personalised to this context.

02 — Personalised Welcome

The chat opens with suggested question chips tailored to the expat's exact situation — school search for children, visa status for immigration-in-progress assignments, housing for those needing temporary accommodation.

03 — Instant AI Answers

GPT-4o responds with policy-scoped guidance specific to the assignment type. Answers reference the expat's profile and are bounded by policy. No out-of-scope guidance is given — ever.

04 — Actions Tracked

Every task is visible with its owner, deadline, and completion status. Overdue items are flagged prominently and injected into the AI context so chat responses are action-aware. The 'due this week' bar surfaces urgency at a glance.

05 — PBP Oversight

The GM and PBP team sees all expat profiles, action completion stats, and overdue counts from a single view. They can simulate any expat's experience in one click to understand exactly what guidance that person is receiving.

What we are validating

Are the policy boundaries correctly enforced per assignment type?

They simulate real expat profiles (LTA, STA, IA, Commuter) and test live AI chat responses.

Feedback is collected per scenario: answer quality, missing coverage, wrong scope, escalation accuracy.

Each feedback cycle directly informs the next iteration of agents, prompts, and FAQ coverage.
