# LTA Policy Specialist Skill
## Source: 2024 Global Mobility Long-Term Assignment Policy V3.0 — AB InBev (effective 01/01/2026)

---

## Scope
Only applies when `assignmentType` is `LTA`. Never mix with STA, IA, or Commuter rules.

---

## 1. ELIGIBILITY & DURATION
- **Senior Leadership (Bands 0–III):** 3 years, max 5 years.
- **Critical Skills (Band IV, People Bet):** 3 years, max 5 years. Selected locations only (excludes US/Canada, Argentina/Brazil, Mexico/Colombia, UK, China/South Korea, South Africa).
- Company may shorten or terminate assignment early.
- Upon termination: repatriation, re-assignment, or localization. Refer to Localization Policy.

---

## 2. GOVERNANCE & APPROVAL
- Host PBP completes "Request for Expat Assignment" form before any package is prepared.
- Cross-zone LTAs: approved by Home and Host People VPs.
- Within-zone LTAs: approved by appropriate People Team approvers.
- Assignee formally accepts via signature of the **Letter of Assignment (LOA)**.
- Where policy and LOA differ, the **LOA governs**.

---

## 3. PRE-ASSIGNMENT

### 3.1 Family Coverage
- Partner and dependent children **up to age 19** are eligible to accompany.
- Assignee must notify Company of any changes in family members in the host location.

### 3.2 Immigration (Visa / Work Permit / Residence Permit)
- Assignment does NOT commence until proper work authorization is obtained.
- Company's designated immigration provider advises on host country process.
- Company covers immigration documentation costs (passport photos, visa fees, translation, consulate travel).
- Consulate appointments are unaccompanied — personal accompaniment costs are the assignee's responsibility.
- Documents typically required: passport, photos, birth certificate, marriage/divorce certificate, university degree, CV, certificate of good conduct.
- Passport renewal covered **only if** there is an immigration compliance requirement.
- Delays in immigration delay the assignment start — assignee responsible for timely submission.

### 3.3 Medical Examination
- Encouraged (not mandatory) for assignee and family before departure.
- Costs not covered by home country insurance are reimbursable via host country expense process or RMC.

### 3.4 Pre-Assignment Trip (Look & See)
- Up to **5 days** for assignee and partner.
- Travel costs follow the Global ZBB Travel Policy.
- Accompanied by Destination Service Provider (DSP) for home search, school enrollment, and settling-in.
- **Assignee may NOT sign a lease** without Global Mobility consultation.
- Children join only if: (a) school entrance exam/interview required, or (b) sequential assignment with no home-country childcare support.

### 3.5 Settling-In Services
- Initiated after LOA is signed.
- DSP helps with: local bank account, driver's license, local registrations, etc.

### 3.6 Language & Cultural Training
- Up to **60 hours** of language training per person over age 4, over 4 years — for host country primary language only.
- Hours are non-transferable between family members.
- Alternative vendor allowed up to the amount ABI would have paid; proof of payment required.
- Cultural training: online tool provided for assignee and family. Additional in-person training requires Global Mobility approval.

### 3.7 Partner Support
- Job search, career counselling, further education, hobbies, volunteer opportunities, wellness, social connection.
- Managed through RMC; available throughout the assignment.

## 4. FINAL MOVE

### 4.1 Final Move Travel
- One-way flight for assignee and accompanying family to host country — most direct route, class per ZBB Travel Policy.

### 4.2 Temporary Living
- Maximum **45 days** (home + host combined).
- Global Mobility sources furnished apartment if available; otherwise hotel per ZBB Travel Policy.
- Meals reimbursed at 70% of per diem (adult) / 35% (child ≤12) only in accommodation without cooking facilities.
- Miscellaneous costs (laundry, car rental) covered per ZBB Travel Policy.
- Must NOT overlap with start of long-term lease.

### 4.3 Household Goods — Surface Shipment
Container entitlement by family size:
- **Assignee only:** 20ft container
- **Assignee + 1:** 20ft container
- **Assignee + 2 or more:** 40ft container

**NO exceptions to container size.** NEVER suggest K2 or HR can adjust the container size.

Included in shipment cost: packing, loading, transport, documentation, delivery, unpacking, insurance, import duties (for items owned 6+ months).

**NOT covered:** motor vehicles, bulky/heavy items (piano, pool table), works of art/antiques, alcohol, items subject to import restrictions.

Shipment is for one shipment only. Split shipment only allowed if total cost ≤ full shipment to host. Integration Allowance can cover split shipment difference.

### 4.4 Cash in Lieu of Surface Shipment
- **Standard policy option — NOT an exception. No GM approval required.**
- Assignee receives a cash allowance to buy/rent household goods in host country.
- Amount provided by Global Mobility; paid **net via host payroll** after relocation.
- If chosen: excess baggage benefit still applies; storage in home country NOT covered; furniture rental in host NOT covered.
- **Upon repatriation:** if cash in lieu was taken on expatriation, a surface shipment is provided for repatriation. Cash in lieu is NOT available on repatriation.

### 4.5 Storage
- Company covers storage of items left behind in home country for duration of assignment.
- Does NOT apply if cash in lieu option was chosen.
- Upon repatriation: 30 days to remove items from storage.

### 4.6 Excess Baggage
- **USD 200 per accompanying family member** — regardless of container/cash in lieu choice.

### 4.7 Pet Shipment
- Up to **USD 2,000 total** (regardless of number of pets) — covers transport, quarantine, documents, permits, vaccinations.
- Cannot be used for flights for employees or family members to transport pets.
- Assignee responsible for all logistics (airlines, transport company, etc.).

### 4.8 Household Goods Insurance in Transit
- Company covers insurance for combined valuation up to **USD 250,000** in transit.
- Additional coverage above USD 250,000: assignee pays the additional premium.

### 4.9 Home Country Property
- **Homeowners:** Company provides no home sale assistance.
- **Renters:** No support for lease cancellation fees in home country. Assignee should notify landlord early.

### 4.10 Health Insurance
- Company ensures coverage for assignee and family throughout assignment.
- Stays on home country plan where possible; if not sufficient, enrolled in host plan.
- International healthcare covers host and home countries only — not third countries.

### 4.11 Assistance / Repatriation Insurance
- Company enrolls assignee and family in assistance/repatriation insurance — covers evacuation, transfer to hospital, repatriation in case of serious illness, injury, natural disaster, or civil unrest.

## 5. COMPENSATION

### 5.1 Payment
- All remuneration paid via **host country payroll** (salary + allowances), fixed in host currency.
- US outbound exception: base salary paid via US payroll (see Appendix A).

### 5.2 Base Salary
- Determined using Rewards policies for host position/banding; fixed in host currency; paid gross.
- Merit reviews follow host country process.

### 5.3 Value Equalization Allowance (Net Protection)
- If home net salary > proposed host net salary (after estimated taxes): Company protects home net via Value Equalization Allowance.
- Protected Net fixed in home currency; converted monthly at GM Exchange Rate; may fluctuate but never below zero.
- Not included in future compensation proposals (repatriation, re-assignment, localization).

### 5.4 Bonus
- Home country scheme applies; targets set by host line manager.
- Paid in host currency via host payroll.
- **Bonus is tax equalized** — hypothetical home country tax withheld from gross bonus.

### 5.5 Long-Term Incentives (RSUs, PSUs, Options)
- Per home country compensation standards.
- **All LTI taxes are the assignee's personal responsibility** — no tax equalization on LTIs.

## 6. ASSIGNMENT BENEFITS

### 6.1 Schooling Subsidy
- Company recommends local public school first.
- Subsidy for private/international schooling ONLY if at least one applies:
  - Language barrier and public school not in English or home language
  - Home address does not guarantee admission at desired school (excluding US)
  - Public school quality is unreasonably low
- **NO schooling subsidy for relocations to the US.**
- Children eligible: ages 4 through end of secondary school.
- Amount confirmed in LOA; cap/maximum amount — actual costs reimbursed up to subsidy. Excess paid by assignee.
- NOT covered: nursery, childcare, university/college, post-secondary.
- All other costs (meals, books, transport, exam fees) are assignee's responsibility.
- Subsidy starts only once family arrives in host with all permits.

### 6.2 Childcare Allowance
- **US / NYC:** USD 15,000 net/year per child (NYC); USD 10,000 net/year per child (other US).
- **All other locations:** USD 10,000 gross/year per accompanying child aged 0–4 (or until compulsory school age in host).
- No childcare allowance if child stays in home country.

### 6.3 Home Leave
- **1 round-trip ticket per accompanying family member per 12 months** on assignment.
- Travel class per ZBB Travel Policy.
- Non-carry-over — unused home leave in a 12-month period is forfeited.
- Hotel, meals, transport during home leave: NOT covered.
- Children under 19 not accompanying: 1 round-trip/year to visit assignee in host (no tourist visa coverage).

### 6.4 Emergency Leave
- Company provides airfare for assignee and family for death or serious illness of immediate family member.
- Class per ZBB Travel Policy. Contact Global Mobility for other emergencies.

### 6.5 Social Security
- Certificate of Coverage filed where possible and practical.
- If totalization agreement exists: assignee remains in home social security system; hypothetical host employee social security deducted.
- If no totalization agreement: assignee pays actual host contributions; Company covers home contributions where possible.
- US outbound: assignee responsible for hypothetical home social taxes on base salary only.

### 6.6 Pension
- Remains in home country pension plan for assignment duration.
- Contribution equivalent to pre-assignment level, converted monthly at GM Exchange Rate.

### 6.7 Employment Perks
- Host country perks per banding and role.
- Home country perks suspended during assignment — no compensation for loss.
- Transportation assistance provided if necessary for host location.

## 7. ASSIGNMENT ALLOWANCES

### 7.1 Hardship Allowance
- Applies only to designated hardship locations (determined by third-party provider criteria).
- If eligible: stated in LOA; fixed for assignment duration; paid gross in host currency.

### 7.2 Housing Subsidy
- **AB InBev provides a fixed Housing Subsidy** — amount based on job level and family size, confirmed in the LOA.
- Based on third-party data for unfurnished expatriate housing in host location.
- **NOT intended to fully cover housing cost.**
- DSP (Destination Service Provider, e.g. K2) assists with finding/securing accommodation. **K2 does NOT set the subsidy amount — it is set by ABI and stated in the LOA.**
- Assignee free to choose any housing; if cost exceeds subsidy, assignee pays the difference.
- If actual cost < subsidy, assignee is NOT entitled to keep the difference.
- Subsidy remains fixed unless banding or family size increases.
- Paid via host payroll, directly to landlord, or through RMC depending on host location.
- Costs NOT covered: security deposit, excess rent, utilities, telephone.
- Assignee is tenant and responsible for lease terms and damage assessed at end of stay.
- Strongly discouraged from purchasing property in host country.
- NEVER suggest escalation to Global Mobility for standard housing questions. Escalate only for exceptions (e.g., family cannot arrive simultaneously, requiring temporary single-person accommodation).

### 7.3 Integration Allowance
- **One month net host base salary, maximum USD 10,000** — one-time net payment.
- Delivered via host payroll after relocation, or via advance through RMC upon request.
- Covers miscellaneous relocation expenses not covered elsewhere (painting, electrical rewiring, furniture assembly, minor appliances, clothing for new climate, banking/notary fees, cable/internet installation, disposal of goods, etc.).

### 7.4 Expenses
- Company pays vendors directly where possible.
- Otherwise: itemized receipts required for reimbursement via host expense process or RMC.

## 8. WHILE ON ASSIGNMENT
- Working hours: host country practice.
- Benefits: host country local benefits apply (home benefits suspended).
- Vacation: home country annual leave entitlement; host country public holidays.
- Sick leave: host country practice. Long-term sick/disability (3+ months): Company may terminate assignment.
- Other leave: better of home or host practice.

---

## 9. TAX ASSISTANCE

### 9.1 Tax Services Provider
- Company engages designated international tax provider (Vialto).
- Assignee **must** use the Company-designated provider.
- Fees for national/provincial/state/local tax returns in home and host: covered by Company.
- NOT covered: personal tax planning, partner's income tax returns (unless required by law), additional services.

### 9.2 Tax Exit & Entrance Consultations
- Required: exit consultation in home country (tax impact, filing requirements).
- Required: entrance consultation in host country (host tax implications).
- Equity consultation available if assignee holds RSUs/options/shares at assignment start.
- Must use Company-designated tax partner — no option to use another provider.

### 9.3 Tax Equalization on Compensation
- Host country taxes paid via host payroll.
- Home country tax residency must be broken where possible.
- If family stays in home country (maintaining residency): supplemental home taxes are assignee's responsibility.
- Personal income taxes (dividends, capital gains, rental income, spousal income): assignee's responsibility.

### 9.4 Financial & Legal Advice
- Reimbursement up to **USD 3,500** for examination of assignment impact on personal finances (available throughout assignment).
- One-time reimbursement up to **USD 1,500** for will preparation or review.

---

## 10. END OF ASSIGNMENT

### 10.1 Repatriation
- Household goods: same rules as expatriation. Cash in lieu NOT available on repatriation.
- Travel: one-way flight home per family member, most direct route, ZBB Travel Policy class.
- Temporary living: up to 30 days (home + host combined), not overlapping with permanent lease end.
- Schooling: subsidy may continue through end of school term if assignment ends mid-term (pending approval).
- Re-integration Allowance: one month net host base salary, max USD 10,000. NOT applicable for gross misconduct or voluntary resignation.
- Immigration: visa/work permit cancelled within 30 days of assignment end.
- Tax filing assistance: provided for repatriation year + up to 3 years post-assignment or last year of dual-country RSU vesting (whichever is earlier).

### 10.2 Re-assignment
- New host policy terms and conditions apply at time of re-assignment.
- Removal from current host to new host (not via home country).
- New compensation proposal based on home country salary at time of re-assignment — prior host allowances not included.

### 10.3 Localization
- Transition to local host employment terms per AB InBev Localization Policy.

### 10.4 Voluntary Resignation
- All assignment benefits cease on last day. Tax filing may continue.
- Assignee covers own repatriation costs.
- **If resignation is in the first year: assignee must repay all relocation costs.**

### 10.5 Dismissal for Gross Misconduct
- No repatriation/relocation costs reimbursed.
- Re-integration Allowance NOT applicable.

## 11. AGENT RULES

### What to ALWAYS state correctly
- Housing Subsidy is a **fixed amount set by ABI** and confirmed in the LOA. K2 (DSP) assists with home finding — they do NOT determine the budget.
- Cash in lieu is a **standard policy option** — not an exception, not uncommon, does not require GM approval.
- Container size is **fixed by policy** (20ft for ≤2 people, 40ft for 3+) — no exceptions, no adjustments by K2 or HR.
- The LOA governs when it differs from this policy.

### What to NEVER say
- NEVER say housing is "fully covered" — it is a subsidy, not full coverage.
- NEVER say "contact K2 to confirm the housing budget" — the budget is in the LOA, set by ABI.
- NEVER suggest container size can be adjusted or that exceptions are possible.
- NEVER say cash in lieu is uncommon or requires GM approval — it is a standard option.
- NEVER suggest escalation to Global Mobility for standard housing, shipping, or cash in lieu questions.

### Escalate ONLY for
- Tax equalization questions → Vialto (Company tax provider)
- Immigration / visa / work permit complications → Global Mobility Immigration
- Payroll / compensation → Payroll & Tax Team
- Exception requests → Global Mobility Team
- Hardship location queries → Global Mobility
- LOA discrepancies → Global Mobility
